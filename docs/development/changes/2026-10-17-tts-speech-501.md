# Change: text-to-speech spike and `/v1/audio/speech` (ALK-US-0058)

Spike result and route for [[ALK-US-0058]] (tasks ALK-T-0161, ALK-T-0162) / [[ALK-SP-0005]].R4.
Decision: [[0003-tts-engine]]. Builds on [[2026-10-17-whisper-stt]] (module `audio`) and
[[plan/alkahest-v1-plan]].

## What

- ADR 0003 records the evaluation: Piper (GPL maintained line; archived MIT line linking GPL
  espeak-ng), Kokoro through ONNX (GPL phonemizer), sherpa-onnx (GPL espeak data), OuteTTS
  (CC-BY-SA model), and llama.cpp `llama-tts` with Qwen3-TTS (Apache-2.0) or Pocket TTS
  (CC-BY-4.0). `llama-tts` is the only licence-clean option, and it is a one-shot CLI that needs
  a reference recording for the voice; `llama-server` b11669 has no TTS route (checked with
  `--help`). No engine is implemented; the decision asks the product owner whether to add a
  one-shot engine kind plus a voice library.
- `alkahest-api::audio::speech`: `POST /v1/audio/speech` validates the JSON body (400 without
  `model` or `input`) and then answers **501** in the OpenAI error shape (`code =
  not_implemented`) with a message pointing at the ADR.

## Verification

`crates/alkahest-api/tests/audio.rs::speech_is_a_clear_501_until_an_engine_exists`.
`llama-tts --help`, `llama-server --help` of b11669 and `tools/tts/README.md` of the tag were read
for the evaluation; model sizes and licences come from the Hugging Face API. Nothing was downloaded
beyond the 17 MB llama.cpp archive, deleted afterwards. `scripts/ci-local.sh` passes.
