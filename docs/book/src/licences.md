# Licences

Alkahest is released under the **MIT licence**, copyright 2026 Digio. The licence text is in the
`LICENSE` file of the repository and of every package.

## Third-party software

- **Rust dependencies**: all licences are listed in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES`, generated by
  `cargo xtask notice` and checked in CI. No GPL, LGPL or AGPL component is shipped
  (`cargo deny check` enforces it). The `NOTICE` files that Apache-2.0 dependencies require are
  reproduced in `NOTICE`.
- **Engines** run as separate processes and keep their own licences: llama.cpp, the PrismML
  fork, whisper.cpp and stable-diffusion.cpp are MIT; vLLM is Apache-2.0. Their licence texts
  are copied next to every installed engine and shipped in `packaging/licenses/`.
- **Fonts** of the desktop app: Schibsted Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1,
  embedded unmodified; the licence texts ship with the desktop packages.
- **GPUI and gpui-kit** (desktop app): Apache-2.0.

## Models

Models are not part of Alkahest and carry their own licences, shown by
`alkahest show <model> --license`. Check them before commercial use; gated Hugging Face
repositories require accepting their terms with your account.

## Text-to-speech

No text-to-speech engine is shipped because no licence-clean option was found (the usual
phonemizers are GPL); see `docs/development/decisions/0003-tts-engine.md` in the repository.
