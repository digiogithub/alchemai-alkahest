Advanced · snapshot limits
Limits protect against oversized or hostile catalogs; a sync that exceeds one fails and keeps the previous snapshot. Saved in [catalog_limits]. Due catalogs refresh in the background every 10 min; ALKAHEST_CATALOG_AUTO_SYNC=0 turns that off.

Private catalogs

Tokens live in the OS keyring (alkahest / catalog:<name>) or in ALKAHEST_CATALOG_TOKEN_<NAME>. They are never shown, logged or written to config.toml, and are sent only to the repository's host after it asks for credentials.
